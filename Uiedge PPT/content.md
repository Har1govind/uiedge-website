### Slide 04 — Our 9-Step Delivery Process

* **Eyebrow:** `04 / PROCESS`
* **Headline:** A system, not a freelancer.
* **Sub-line:** Nine structured steps — each tied directly to a business rationale:
  1. **Discovery:** Align on business goals and unit economics before pushing pixels.
  2. **Research:** Understand real user behavior and pain points, not assumptions.
  3. **Strategy:** Map information architecture and conversion flow.
  4. **Wireframing:** Validate structure low-fidelity — solving layout early saves engineering cost.
  5. **UI Design:** Deliver modern, accessible, conversion-focused interfaces.
  6. **Prototype:** Test interactive Figma flows before writing single line of code.
  7. **Testing:** Usability testing and WCAG accessibility compliance verification.
  8. **Dev Handoff:** Structured design tokens, specs, and frontend integration support.
  9. **Optimization:** Monitor post-launch analytics and optimize conversion funnel.
* **Visual Component:** 3x3 step matrix with mono numerals (`01` through `09`) and glowing borders.

### Slide 05 — Case Study 01 (Hospitality) — Challenge & Strategy

* **Eyebrow:** `05 / CASE STUDY — HOSPITALITY`
* **Headline:** A booking site guests didn't trust.
* **Chips:** `Hospitality` · `8-week sprint` · `UX/UI + Frontend Build`
* **Content:**
  * **Context:** A boutique hospitality brand in Kerala had a 5-year-old website that no longer reflected their premium physical property.
  * **Challenge:** Slow page speeds (>6s load), cluttered 9-item navigation, and buried booking CTAs caused heavy drop-offs to OTAs.
  * **Goal:** Create a high-converting, lightning-fast site (<2s) that earns trust instantly and drives direct bookings.
  * **Approach:** Streamlined navigation from 9 to 5 items, rebuilt visual hierarchy around immersive imagery, and introduced a sticky single-click booking widget.
* **Visual Component:** Split layout with problem teardown on left and browser frame mockup on right.
* **Footer Strip:** `uiedge.in` · `admin@uiedge.in` · `05 / 16`
